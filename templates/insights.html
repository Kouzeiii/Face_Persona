Evaluation Methodology: Held-out Test R² evaluates predictive performance on an independent, unobserved 20% test partition without preprocessing data leakage (StandardScaler and PCA are fitted strictly on the training partition). Secondary OOB R² is an internal diagnostic computed during training on out-of-bag samples of the training partition only, distinct from test generalization.
